A single drip can waste several gallons a day, enough to show up on a bill within a cycle. A leak under the sink does a different kind of damage, soaking the cabinet floor and growing mold in the dark. Both are cheaper to fix early.

The brand matters. Some faucets use a standard cartridge, others use a proprietary one that we order or replace outright if the finish is already worn. We tell you which road you are on before we open the handle.

Repair or replace the faucet

A faucet with a sound body and a worn cartridge is a repair. A faucet with a pitted finish, a cracked body or discontinued parts is a replacement. If you are upgrading, we install the new faucet, test the supply lines and check the angle stops while we are under there. Larger fixture swaps and new supply runs fall under plumbing installation.

Low flow and mineral buildup

Weak flow from one faucet usually means a clogged aerator, the small screen at the tip. Unscrew it, rinse out the grit and reinstall. If every faucet in the house is weak, the cause is upstream, such as a partly closed valve, a failing pressure regulator or a corroded supply line. DeKalb County's water system sets the pressure at the street, so a house-wide drop is worth a call to us and, if the neighbors have it too, a call to the utility.

Things you can do before we arrive

  • ✓Close the angle stop under the sink, turning it clockwise until the drip stops.
  • ✓Unscrew the aerator and rinse it if flow is weak.
  • ✓Empty the cabinet and place a towel or tray under the pipes.
  • ✓Note the faucet brand and model, often stamped under the handle or on the box, which speeds up parts.

Outdoor faucets and hose bibs

A hose bib that drips at the handle needs new packing, and one that leaks inside the wall after a cold snap may have a split pipe behind it. We check for that before closing it. If you see a wet spot on the interior wall near an outdoor spigot, call us and read about leak detection in the meantime.

What affects faucet repair cost

Standards and sources

Plumbing work in Georgia follows the state minimum plumbing code, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Georgia amendments. Ask the City of Tucker permit team which edition and which local rules apply to your address. Plumbers are licensed through the Secretary of State's Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers, and you can ask for a license number. When you shop for toilets and faucets, the EPA WaterSense label marks models that meet its water efficiency criteria, and WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons per flush or less.
