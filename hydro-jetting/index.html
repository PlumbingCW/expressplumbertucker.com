Emergency Hydro Jetting in Tucker, GA

If a plumber snaked your drain and it clogged again a few weeks later, the cable probably punched a hole through the blockage without removing it. Hydro jetting washes the pipe wall clean with high-pressure water, and our 24/7 emergency line can schedule it when a backup will not wait.

The drain that clears, then clogs again

A cable opens a channel through a clog. It does not scrape the full circumference of the pipe, so grease, scale and fine roots remain on the wall and build back. People describe it as "they snaked it and it came back in a month." That pattern is the signal to move from cabling to jetting.

Jetting sends water at high pressure through a hose with a nozzle that points backward as well as forward. The forward jets break up the blockage and the rear jets pull the hose through and scrub the pipe wall. The debris flushes downstream, so the line ends up clean, not merely open.

When a backup is the emergency

A main line that is backing up into a tub or floor drain is an emergency, because sewage in the house is a health matter as well as a repair. If the line is solidly blocked, we may clear a path first with a cable and follow with jetting so the cleaning is complete.

We run a camera before jetting when the situation allows. It shows whether the pipe is sound enough for pressure, and it keeps us from blasting water into a line that is already cracked. After jetting we run the camera again so you see the clean pipe, not just our word for it.

Roots, grease and scale: what jetting removes

Roots grow into joints and hairline cracks looking for water and nutrients. Jetting with the right nozzle cuts and flushes fine roots, though large root masses often need a mechanical cutter first. Because roots grow back, we talk about the likelihood of regrowth and whether a repair at the entry point would stop it.

Grease behaves like candle wax inside a cool pipe. Scale from mineral deposits narrows cast iron over decades. Jetting handles both. If the pipe wall shows deep pitting or flaking on the camera, we tell you that too, because you may be looking at sewer line repair sooner than you hoped.

Good candidates for jetting

  • ✓A line that has clogged more than twice in a year despite cabling.
  • ✓Slow drains throughout the house with no single fixture to blame.
  • ✓A restaurant, laundry or multi-unit building with heavy grease in its lines. See commercial plumbing for light commercial scope.
  • ✓A cast iron line with visible scale on the camera.

Jetting and the Tucker sewer connection

Most Tucker addresses connect to the DeKalb County sewer system. Jetting your private side line is normal maintenance, but we do not jet the county main in the street. If the camera shows the blockage is beyond your property line, we document it and point you to DeKalb County Watershed Management. For hands-on clearing short of jetting, see drain cleaning.

What to do before we arrive

  • ✓Stop running water into the blocked line, including the washer and dishwasher.
  • ✓Locate the cleanout cap and clear a path to it.
  • ✓Keep children and pets away from any room with standing sewage.
  • ✓Do not pour chemical drain cleaners into the line, since they are hazardous near cutting and jetting equipment.

Standards and sources

Plumbing work in Georgia follows the state minimum plumbing code, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Georgia amendments. Ask the City of Tucker permit team which edition and which local rules apply to your address. Plumbers are licensed through the Secretary of State's Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers, and you can ask for a license number.
