Licensing and standards

Georgia licenses plumbers as journeymen and masters through the Secretary of State's Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers. Only licensed master plumbers can operate a plumbing business, and the licensing board does not register companies. Ask any plumber for a license number and verify it. Georgia's minimum plumbing code is built on the International Plumbing Code with Georgia amendments, and permit rules are set locally, so we point you to the City of Tucker permit team for your address.

Where we work

We are based in Tucker and serve Stone Mountain, Clarkston, Lilburn and Norcross. See the full service area for ZIP codes.

How pricing works

You hear the price before work starts. For simple jobs, such as a fixture repair, the dispatcher can often give a figure on the phone. For larger jobs, the plumber diagnoses first and then states the price for the whole scope. If the scope changes, we ask before we proceed. We do not publish price lists on this site, because a figure that ignores your house would be a guess. Each service page lists the factors that move the price, so you can see what matters before you call.

What we will not do

  • Recommend a replacement before we have looked at the cause.
  • Start work before you agree to the price.
  • Publish reviews, ratings or awards that we cannot show.
  • Claim local history or facts we have not checked.
  • Pretend a utility problem is yours, or the reverse.

When something goes wrong

Plumbing work sometimes needs a second visit. A fitting weeps after the pressure comes up, or a drain slows again. Call and say so. We would rather return than leave a problem in your house. For anything that looks like a safety issue, such as gas, sparks or sewage, stop and call the line right away.

How we handle emergencies at night

A night call follows the same steps as a day call. The dispatcher asks what is happening, gives you the first steps to limit damage and sends the plumber who is on duty. The price is stated before work starts, and after-hours work is quoted on the same call. We keep the line staffed so that the first voice you hear is a person, not a recording.

Who we serve

We serve homeowners, landlords, tenants and small businesses. A landlord with a clogged shared line, a restaurant with a floor drain backing up and a family with no hot water have different needs, and we plan the visit around them. If you rent, tell your landlord as well as us, since they often decide on repairs.

Every page on this site is written by our team for Tucker-area homeowners, and we keep to facts we can check. Where a number depends on your house, such as a price or the hardness of your water, we say so and tell you how to find out.
