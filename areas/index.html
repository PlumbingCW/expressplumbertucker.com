Neighborhoods and districts in Tucker

Four parts of Tucker have their own pages: the residential communities of Smoke Rise and Idlewood, the historic center at Downtown Tucker and the commercial district of Northlake.

DeKalb and Gwinnett: why the county matters

Stone Mountain and Clarkston are in DeKalb County, like Tucker. Lilburn and Norcross are in Gwinnett County, where the Department of Water Resources supplies water from Lake Lanier. When a leak or a sewer problem might sit on the utility side, we need to know which utility to call, so the county is the first thing the dispatcher confirms.
