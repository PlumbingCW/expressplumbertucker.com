Toilet overflowing right now

Do not flush again. Lift the tank lid and close the flapper, or turn the shutoff valve at the wall clockwise. Put towels around the base and move rugs out of the way. If the bowl is still rising, the tank is refilling it, and closing that valve is what stops the flow.

If water is also coming up in a tub or shower, the problem is not the toilet. That points to a main line blockage, which belongs under sewer line cleaning, and you should stop using water throughout the house.

A toilet that runs, hisses or flushes weakly

Most running toilets have a flapper that no longer seals. Minerals harden the rubber, and a chain that is too short holds it open a crack. The water then trickles into the bowl and the fill valve keeps topping it up, which raises your bill without making a sound you would call a leak.

A weak flush often comes from mineral buildup in the rim holes under the bowl lip or a tank that is not filling to the line. We clean and adjust before suggesting a new toilet. If the toilet is decades old and cracking, a swap may be the better value, and we cover that choice under plumbing installation.

Rocking toilet and water at the base

A toilet that rocks has a loose flange or worn bolts, and the wax ring beneath it no longer seals. Water on the floor around the base can be sewer water, not clean water, so wear gloves and keep children out. We lift the toilet, inspect the flange and subfloor, set a new seal and tighten it level. If the floor around the base is soft, we tell you before we close it up, since rot under a toilet is a hidden repair that grows.

Before the plumber arrives

  • ✓Close the shutoff valve behind the toilet and keep the lid down.
  • ✓Photograph any water on the floor, which helps if you file a claim.
  • ✓Do not pour drain chemicals into a clogged bowl. They splash back and make the repair hazardous.
  • ✓Keep a plunger away from a bowl that is already overflowing, since it can push water onto the floor.

Toilet trouble in Tucker homes

Tucker homes range from mid-century ranches near Lawrenceville Highway to newer townhomes closer to Northlake Parkway, and the toilets inside them range just as widely. Older houses often have narrow drain lines and a long run to the street, so a stubborn clog may be deeper than the toilet. When we pull a toilet we look at the line beyond it, and if the drain is the cause we point you to drain cleaning rather than keep replacing parts.

Standards and sources

Plumbing work in Georgia follows the state minimum plumbing code, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Georgia amendments. Ask the City of Tucker permit team which edition and which local rules apply to your address. Plumbers are licensed through the Secretary of State's Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers, and you can ask for a license number. When you shop for toilets and faucets, the EPA WaterSense label marks models that meet its water efficiency criteria, and WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons per flush or less.
