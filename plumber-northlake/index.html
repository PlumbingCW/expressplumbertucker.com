What Northlake is

Northlake is the name locals and planners use for a commercial area in the north of Tucker. The City of Tucker comprehensive plan describes it as including Northlake Mall and the surrounding large commercial parcels. The plan designates it a Regional Activity Center and says the city plans to change that to a Town Center designation. The Tucker-Northlake Community Improvement District, a group of commercial property owners, works on the area, and the city has adopted a Tucker-Northlake Livable Centers Initiative update and created new mixed-use zoning for it.

Northlake is a commercial and mixed-use district, so we treat it that way. Apartment communities and houses exist around it, but they are not what this page is about.

Boundaries and mailing addresses

Listings for Northlake properties show both Atlanta (ZIP 30345) and Tucker (ZIP 30084) mailing addresses, so not every Northlake address is in the City of Tucker. On November 3, 2026, voters approved annexing three apartment communities in the Northlake area into Tucker. They are Avana City North, Atlas LaVista Hills and Providence of Northlake. The change takes effect on January 1, 2027. If permits matter for your project, confirm with the city and the county which jurisdiction applies to your address.

Calls from commercial buildings

Restaurants and food service

Grease in drains, floor drains that back up, three-compartment sinks and water heaters that must keep up at peak hours.

Retail and offices

Restroom fixtures, leaks in ceilings, supply valves and small water heaters.

Multi-unit buildings

Shared drain stacks, risers and common water heaters, where one problem can reach several units.

Landlords and managers

Written summaries, scheduling around tenants and clear records for insurers.

Backflow, records and inspections

Many commercial properties have backflow prevention assemblies, which protect the public water supply from contamination and usually need periodic testing by a certified tester. Rules and schedules are set by the water utility and local authorities, so check what applies to your building. We keep a written record of what we find and fix, with photos and camera footage where relevant, so you have it for a landlord, an inspector or an insurer.

Local note: planning changes in the district

Planning documents point to more mixed-use development in Northlake over time. For building owners that means older and newer plumbing sometimes share a block, and tenant changes can bring new demands on drains and water heaters. Tell us the building type and what has changed recently.

Water and sewer for addresses on the public system in DeKalb County come from DeKalb County Watershed Management.
