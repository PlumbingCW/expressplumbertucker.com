Water is pouring: do this now

1

Shut off the main

Turn the main valve clockwise. If it is stuck, use the meter shutoff at the street, and call us.

2

Stay safe

Keep away from electrical outlets in wet rooms. Switch off power at the breaker if water reaches outlets and you can get to the panel dry.

3

Open low faucets

Open a faucet at the lowest level to drain the line and reduce pressure.

4

Photograph and call

Document the damage, then call the 24/7 line. We can usually be there the same day.

Why pipes burst in Georgia

Freezes here are infrequent, which is exactly why pipes are caught unprepared. In the Christmas weekend cold of December 2022, DeKalb County reported a run of water main breaks and a boil water advisory covering parts of the county, and plenty of homeowners learned where their shutoff was the hard way. Pipes in attics, garages, outside walls and crawlspaces are the ones at risk. Insulate them, let a faucet drip on the coldest nights, and know your main valve before you need it.

Prepare before the next cold snap

  • ✓Locate and test your main shutoff once a year. If it is stiff or leaks, have it replaced.
  • ✓Disconnect hoses and cover outdoor faucets before a freeze.
  • ✓Open cabinet doors on exterior walls on the coldest nights.
  • ✓Let a faucet drip at the far end of the house when temperatures fall well below freezing.

That kind of pressure swing can stress old fittings. If the break is between the meter and the house, see water service line repair. If you cannot find the source, leak detection comes first.

Standards and sources

Plumbing work in Georgia follows the state minimum plumbing code, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Georgia amendments. Ask the City of Tucker permit team which edition and which local rules apply to your address. Plumbers are licensed through the Secretary of State's Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers, and you can ask for a license number. Any digging starts with a free call to Georgia 811, at least three business days ahead.
