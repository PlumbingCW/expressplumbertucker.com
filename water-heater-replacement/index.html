When a failed tank is an emergency

A tank that is leaking from its body, flooding a utility room, or leaving a household with no hot water in cold weather is an emergency. Water heaters hold 40 to 50 gallons in a typical home, and a weeping seam can become a flood within hours.

Our crews carry or can source the common sizes, so a same-day swap is realistic for standard replacements. Specialty sizes, tight closets and venting changes can add a day. We will tell you at the first call which category you are in.

4

We fill the tank, purge air, check for leaks, test venting and gas connections, and show you the shutoffs.

Sizing the new heater for your household

A tank that is too small runs out of hot water by the second shower. One that is too large wastes energy keeping extra gallons hot. We size by number of people, number of bathrooms and whether you run a soaker tub, a dishwasher and a washer back to back.

Measure nothing yourself. Tell us what you have, and we will check the nameplate on the old unit and suggest an equal or better size. If you are weighing an upgrade, our water heater installation page covers how a planned installation differs from an emergency swap.

Venting, expansion tanks and code points that matter

A gas water heater has to vent its exhaust through a flue sized for the unit. A replacement that changes fuel type or capacity may require changes to that venting. Most homes also need a thermal expansion tank on the cold supply when a backflow device or pressure reducing valve closes the system, and a pan and drain line where a leak could damage finished space.

Georgia's state minimum plumbing code is built on the International Plumbing Code with Georgia amendments, and local jurisdictions inspect against it. Permit rules vary, so ask the City of Tucker permit team whether your swap needs a permit. Tucker's Building and Permitting office inspects work at different stages, and we can describe what we plan to do so you can ask the right question.

Tank type and size

A 50-gallon gas unit and a hybrid heat pump unit are in different price classes.

Venting changes

A new flue liner or relocated vent adds parts and time.

Code items

Expansion tank, pan, drain line and updated shutoffs are added when the situation calls for them.

Access

Attic and crawlspace installs are slower than garage or closet installs.

Warranty term

Longer manufacturer warranties usually come with higher-grade tanks.

Replacement in Tucker houses: what we see

Tank heaters in Tucker-area homes sit in garages, closets, basements and attics, and each spot changes the job. A unit in an interior closet needs a drain pan and clearance. One in a garage needs protection from impact. An attic unit needs a safe path for the old tank to come down.

Standards and sources

Plumbing work in Georgia follows the state minimum plumbing code, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Georgia amendments. Ask the City of Tucker permit team which edition and which local rules apply to your address. Plumbers are licensed through the Secretary of State's Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers, and you can ask for a license number. Gas piping and venting for water heaters fall under the National Fuel Gas Code, NFPA 54, as adopted locally.
