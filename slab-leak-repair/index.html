Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Tucker, GA

A slab leak is a break in a pipe under or inside a concrete slab. It can waste water for weeks before it shows, and it does not always show at all. Our 24/7 emergency line sends a plumber who confirms and locates the leak before anyone suggests breaking concrete.

If the floor is warm, the sound of running water continues with everything off, or the meter turns, close the main shutoff and call.

Is there water running under my floor?

A warm or hot spot on the floor

A hot water line leaking under the slab can warm the surface above it.

Sound of water with everything off

A faint hiss or rush in a quiet house points to a pressurized leak.

Meter moving with fixtures closed

Confirms water is escaping somewhere on the property.

Damp carpet, lifted flooring or a musty smell

Water has come up through the slab or the slab edge.

A sudden jump in the water bill

Often the first sign, before anything is visible.

Not every house in the Tucker area has a slab foundation. Many have crawlspaces or basements, where leaks are easier to reach. If yours is slab-on-grade, hidden leaks need more careful diagnosis, and we first rule out the simpler explanations: a running toilet, a dripping hose bib or a leak in the line between the meter and the house.

The diagnosis is the main job. We pressure test the supply, listen along the floor with acoustic equipment and use thermal imaging for hot lines. Only then do we say where the leak is likely to be and what the repair options are. See leak detection for the full method.

Why we do not suggest breaking concrete first

Cutting a slab is the most disruptive way to fix a leak. It is sometimes the right answer, but it should follow a diagnosis, not replace one. A reroute through the attic or walls often costs less than reaching a pipe under tile and furniture, and it solves the problem of a line that may fail again. We lay out each route, the disruption it brings and the likely price range, and you choose.

While you wait for the plumber

  • ✓Close the main water shutoff to stop the leak and protect the floor.

Related paths include burst pipe repair for sudden breaks and water service line repair for leaks before the house.

Standards and sources

Plumbing work in Georgia follows the state minimum plumbing code, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Georgia amendments. Ask the City of Tucker permit team which edition and which local rules apply to your address. Plumbers are licensed through the Secretary of State's Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers, and you can ask for a license number. Any digging starts with a free call to Georgia 811, at least three business days ahead.
