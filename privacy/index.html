Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect when you call us or visit this website, and what we do with it.

Talk to a plumber

Information we collect

When you call, we collect the phone number you call from, the address and details you give about the problem, and a recording of the call. This website has no forms or accounts. Your browser may send us standard technical data such as pages visited and device type.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call or email us instead.

How we use information

We use it to dispatch a plumber, quote and complete the work, improve our service and meet legal and insurance requirements. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We share information with the plumber assigned to your job and with service providers who help us operate, such as phone and hosting providers, only as needed. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic analytics to understand page use. It does not use advertising profiles built from your visit.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it where the law allows, or opt out of future contact. Email contact@expressplumbertucker.com or call (770) 214-4545.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the bottom of the page shows the last revision: 2026-11-10.
