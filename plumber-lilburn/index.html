Plumbing in a Gwinnett city built along US 29

Lilburn was founded in 1890 when the Seaboard railroad arrived, and it was incorporated in 1910. US 29 runs through the center of town, with houses and businesses spread along it and the side streets. At about 950 feet, it is lower than Tucker, and the slope toward creeks shapes how lines run.

Because Lilburn is in Gwinnett County, its public water and sewer is not DeKalb's. Gwinnett County's Department of Water Resources names Lake Lanier as its sole water source, with about 4,000 miles of distribution pipe and over 3,000 miles of sewer. That is useful context when a bill spikes or pressure drops, and we tell you which utility to call if the problem is outside your property line.

Houses of different ages

Houses off the older streets near the center often have original lines, while newer neighborhoods have plastic supply and drain pipes. A water heater near its second decade is a common reason for calls. If yours is older and leaking at the base, read about water heater installation and plan a replacement before it floods the closet.

Local note: the mandir and the temple traffic

The BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir stands about two miles southwest of the center of Lilburn. Large gatherings put heavy demand on restrooms and kitchens in the surrounding area.

If you manage a venue or a larger property near a regular event site, plan for a plumber visit before the busy season and keep a camera inspection report on file.

City Park and the neighborhoods around it

Lilburn City Park anchors the town, and neighborhoods around it include a mix of older houses and newer homes. When the ground is soaked after heavy rain, a yard that stays wet may point to a leak, and our leak detection page explains how we tell rain from a broken line.

Questions Lilburn callers ask most

Callers from Lilburn often ask whether a problem is theirs or the county's. The test is the meter and the property line. If the meter moves with your house main closed, the leak is in the line between the meter and the house. If sewage is backing up and your neighbors have the same trouble, the public main is the likelier cause.

They also ask how quickly a plumber can reach them. The dispatcher gives an arrival window when you call, and same-day visits are common when crews are available. Tell the dispatcher your street and what you see, and the call goes faster.

Standards and sources

Plumbing work in Georgia follows the state minimum plumbing code, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Georgia amendments. Ask the City of Tucker permit team which edition and which local rules apply to your address. Plumbers are licensed through the Secretary of State's Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers, and you can ask for a license number. Water and sewer service is provided by the county utility for your address, and we confirm which one applies.
